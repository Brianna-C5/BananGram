On your turn, click any face-down tile to flip it. Revealed tiles stay visible on the board.

Available letters in center

Claim, extend, or steal a word

Tap any player’s word, including your own, to extend it using center letters. If it belongs to another player, a successful extension steals it. To claim using center letters only, deselect the word. Everyone may submit at any time.

Using center letters only

Up to 8 players. Players take turns revealing tiles; everyone can race to claim a word at any time. Extensions and steals require every letter of the selected word and at least one new center letter. Simple plurals and verb-tense changes are forbidden. The first valid claim received by the server wins. Disconnected seats cannot currently be reclaimed.
